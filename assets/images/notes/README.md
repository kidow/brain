# Note illustrations

Style A: white background, clean charcoal contours, limited blue accents, only the objects and relationships needed to explain the lesson. Keep text inside images to short labels. Do not add decorative props, effects, textures, frames, or unrelated symbols.

- `manifest.json` maps each illustration to its note, section, alternative text, and caption.
- SVGs are editable, self-contained diagrams created for these notes. Their numeric examples and curves are schematic unless the caption identifies a calculation.
- PNGs are original illustrations generated with the built-in image generation tool. `clothing.png` and `motion-ru.png` are the approved previews; the other illustrations follow the same direction.
- Reuse an existing asset when the teaching concept is the same across languages. Keep language-specific grammar in the surrounding note and caption.
- Preserve the article text and lesson order. Place illustrations beside the section they explain.
- Images use explicit dimensions, lazy loading, descriptive alternative text, and the shared `note-illustrations.css`. Wide SVG diagrams can scroll horizontally on small screens; print removes the minimum width.

## PNG prompt briefs

All briefs use the style above and exclude decorative elements, long prose, backgrounds, and visual effects.

- `clothing.png`: one person putting on a T-shirt, trousers, and a hat; highlight the relevant garment; labels 着る / 履く / かぶる.
- `motion-ru.png`: home-to-school movement on foot, one current direction versus a repeated round trip example; labels идти / ходить and short Korean cues.
- `cooking.png`: food on a grill, in simmering water, in frying oil, and on a steamer tray above water; labels 焼く / 煮る / 揚げる / 蒸す.
- `door-action.png`: a person opening a door beside the resulting open doorway without a person; blue on the door leaf, one functional swing arrow, no labels.
- `reflexive.png`: washing one's own face versus washing another person's face; water is the only blue accent, no labels.
- `aspiration.png`: the same face holding paper near the lips; an aspirated puff bends the paper, unaspirated release leaves it upright; labels p · 유기음 / b · 무기음.
